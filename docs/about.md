# About Bata Maru ri

**Bata Maru ri** (*बातां मरु री* — "Tales of the Desert Land") is an evolving digital notebook dedicated to Rajasthani folklore, oral narratives, and academic Rajasthani studies.

## Current Focus

At this initial stage, the site is focused on **summarizing local Rajasthani stories**—recording plots, regional variants, motifs, and sources as a foundation for broader study.

## Future Scope

Over time, this space will expand to include:

- Essays and historiographical notes on Rajasthani literature and oral epics
- Annotated bibliographies and manuscript/archival references
- A working glossary of *Ḍiṅgaḷ*, *Mārwāṛī*, and regional folk terms
